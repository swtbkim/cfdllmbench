## Prompt (`usr_requirement`)

> do a laminar dam break with obstacle simulation. use interFoam solver. The domain extent is 1x1x1. Use 32 grid points in all directions. The obstacle is a block occupying the box from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25) m; remove these cells from the mesh and treat the obstacle surface as a wall. At the start, water fills the box from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) m and air fills the rest of the domain; gravity is (0, 0, -9.81) m/s^2. Use density of air to be 1 kg/m3 and density of water to be 1000 kg/m3. Take the viscosity of air to be 1.48e-05 and viscosity of water to be 0.001. The simulation runs from t=0s to t=0.1s with a timestep of 0.001s and a write interval of 0.02.
