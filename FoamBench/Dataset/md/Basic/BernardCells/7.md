## Prompt (`usr_requirement`)

> Perform a 2D Bernard Cell simulation using OpenFOAM buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam solver. The computational domain spans 9 m x 1 m in x and y, meshed with 90 x 10 cells, with a single cell of 2 m thickness in z and empty front and back patches. The simulation begins at t=0 seconds and runs until t = 1000 seconds with a time step of 1 second, and results are written at intervals of every 50 seconds. The bottom wall (floor) has a temperature of 310 K and the top wall (ceiling) has a temperature of 295 K; the side walls are adiabatic (zero-gradient temperature).
