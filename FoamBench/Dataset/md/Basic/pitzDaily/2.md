## Prompt (`usr_requirement`)

> do a Reynolds-Averaged Simulation (RAS) pitzdaily simulation. Use PIMPLE algorithm. The domain is a 2D millimeter-scale channel geometry. Use the standard OpenFOAM pitzDaily geometry and mesh. Boundary conditions specify a fixed velocity of 15m/s at the inlet (left),fixed-value pressure of 0 at the outlet (right) with zero-gradient velocity there, and no-slip conditions for walls. Use timestep of 0.0001 and output every 0.01. Finaltime is 0.3. use nu value of 1e-05. Use the standard k-epsilon RAS model with k = 0.375 m^2/s^2 and epsilon = 14.855 m^2/s^3 at the inlet and as initial values, and standard wall functions (kqRWallFunction, epsilonWallFunction, nutkWallFunction) on upperWall and lowerWall. Do not use more than 13000 cells in your mesh.
