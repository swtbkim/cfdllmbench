## Prompt (`usr_requirement`)

> do a laminar offsetcylinder simulation. Use PIMPLE algorithm with a generalized Newtonian model and Cross-Power-Law viscosity for momentum transport, characterized by specific parameters nuInf=10, m=0.4, n=3. Boundary conditions specify a fixed velocity of 3m/s at the inlet (left),fixed-value pressure of 0 at the outlet (right) with zero-gradient velocity there, and no-slip conditions for walls, including the cylinder surface. Use timestep of 0.0025 and output every 0.05. Finaltime is 0.5. use a Newtonian transport model with nu value of 1e-02 in transportProperties. Do not use more than 1800 cells in your mesh.
