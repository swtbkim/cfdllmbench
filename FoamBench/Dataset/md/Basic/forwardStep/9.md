## Prompt (`usr_requirement`)

> do a laminar, compressible flow over a forward-facing step using the rhoCentralFoam solver. Boundary conditions include a fixed velocity of 2.5m/s and temperature of 1 K at the inlet and slip conditions on the obstacle. Use timestep of 0.002 and output every 0.1. Finaltime is 4. Do not use more than 17000 cells in your mesh.

    U
    {
        solver          smoothSolver;

        tolerance       1e-10;
        relTol          0;

        relTol          0;
